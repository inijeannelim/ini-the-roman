LINK EDITING GUIDE — V36

1. Open index.html in VS Code.
2. Search (Cmd+F) for: LINK HERE
3. Replace PASTE_YOUR_LINK_HERE in the href attribute with the relevant URL.
4. All press coverage and gifting tiles have clickable images AND captions.
5. Existing Proper Magazine links and Denim Dudes post URL are prefilled.
6. For a tile without a URL yet, clicking it will not navigate until you replace the placeholder.

Example:
<a href="PASTE_YOUR_LINK_HERE" ...>
becomes
<a href="https://www.instagram.com/p/EXAMPLE/" ...>

Search 'earned-tile' to locate all coverage and gifting cards.
