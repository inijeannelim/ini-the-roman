SO, WHY
THE ROMANS?

Since I haven't been invited for an interview (yet), I thought I'd start one myself.

Q / 01

You weren't invited to apply. Why are we reading this?

Because I like making things happen, even when nobody has asked me to. A conventional cover letter felt a little predictable for an agency that says goodbye to boring PR. So here we are.

Q / 02

Why move from in-house to an agency?

Working in-house taught me to understand a brand from the inside out, and to care about the details long after the big idea has been approved. Now I want to bring that perspective to different brands, collaborate with people who think differently and keep learning.

Q / 03

What would you bring to the team?

A mix of creative thinking, genuine relationships and hands-on execution. I enjoy spotting opportunities, but I also enjoy making the guest list, chasing the assets, solving the last-minute problem and being there when it all comes together.

Q / 04

What kind of work excites you?

Ideas that leave the presentation deck and meet real people. The unexpected invitation, the room full of the right people, the brand connection that feels natural rather than forced.

Q / 05

Freelance, part-time or full-time?

I'm open to all three. The people, the projects and the opportunity to contribute matter most.

Q / 06

What makes you better than AI?

I have gut feelings, we need it to do this job.
